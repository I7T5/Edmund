Switch between Edit and Read

Each document window shows the document one of three ways.

Edit
Formatted text you can change. Markdown characters show when the insertion point is inside them.
Read
The finished page, laid out as it will look when exported. You can’t edit in Read.
Source
Plain Markdown with no formatting hidden. Choose View > Show Source in Editor to turn it on or off.

To switch between Edit and Read, choose View > Show Reader or View > Show Editor, press Command-E, or click the Edit/Read button in the toolbar. The window stays at the same place in the document.

Change the size of text

Choose View > Zoom In (Command-Equal Sign (=)) or View > Zoom Out (Command-Hyphen (-)). Zooming scales the text and the column width together. Choose View > Actual Size (Command-0) to go back.

To change the font or column width for every document, see Appearance settings.

Show or hide the toolbar, format bar, status bar, and inspector

Toolbar
Choose View > Hide Toolbar or View > Show Toolbar. To add buttons such as Format, Checklist, Table, Image, Link, and Share, choose View > Customize Toolbar.
Format bar
Choose View > Show Format Bar.
Status bar
The bar at the bottom of the window shows word and character counts, the line, and the line ending. It hides until you move the pointer over it. To keep it visible, choose View > Always Show Status Bar. To choose what it shows, Control-click it.
Inspector
Choose View > Show Inspector, or click the Inspector button in the toolbar. Outline lists the document’s headings; click one to go to it, or select one and press Return. Info shows the word and character counts, the location, the line, and the line ending.

Write without distraction

Typewriter Scroll
Keeps the line you are typing near the middle of the window. Choose Edit > Typewriter Scroll.
Focus Mode
Dims every line except the ones you have selected or are typing in. Choose Edit > Focus Mode.
