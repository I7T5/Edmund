Key Bindings settings

To open these settings, choose Edmund > Settings, then click Key Bindings, or choose Help > Keyboard Shortcuts.

The list shows Edmund’s menu commands, grouped by menu. Click the disclosure triangle next to a submenu to see its commands.

Change a shortcut

  1. Click in the Key column next to the command.
  2. Press the new keys. A shortcut must include Command or Control; it can also include Option and Shift.

To cancel, press Escape. To remove a shortcut, press Delete.

If the keys are already used by another command, Edmund beeps and names that command below the list. Press different keys, or change the other command first.

Standard macOS commands, such as New, Save, Copy, Paste, Undo, and Quit, keep their usual shortcuts and aren’t listed.

Go back to the original shortcuts

Click Restore Defaults. Every shortcut you changed goes back to the one Edmund started with. See Keyboard shortcuts for the defaults.
