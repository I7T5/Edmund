Find and replace text

Find text

  1. Choose Edit > Find > Find, or press Command-F.
  2. Type in the Find field. Edmund highlights every match and shows how many there are.
  3. To go to the next or previous match, click the arrows, or press Command-G or Shift-Command-G.

To match capital letters exactly or find only whole words, click the magnifying glass in the Find field, then choose Case Sensitive or Whole Words.

Replace text

  1. Choose Edit > Find > Find and Replace, or press Option-Command-F. You can also select the Replace checkbox in the find bar.
  2. Type the new text in the Replace field.
  3. Click Replace to change the current match, or All to change every match.

Click Done to close the find bar.

Find works in Edit only. In Read, the Find commands are dimmed.
