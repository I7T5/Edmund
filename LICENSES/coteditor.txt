Anura and Dendrobates
https://github.com/coteditor/CotEditor

Edmund's `anura`, `anura-dark`, `dendrobates` and `dendrobates-dark` code themes
take their colors from CotEditor's bundled themes of the same names, by 1024jp.
Those theme files carry the license note "Same as CotEditor (Apache, ver.2)".

Only the ten code-scope colors were taken. The originals also carry editor
colors — page, insertion point, selection — which Edmund's code themes do not
have; that is the change made to them.
