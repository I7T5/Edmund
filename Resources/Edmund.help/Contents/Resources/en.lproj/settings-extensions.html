Extensions settings

To open these settings, choose Edmund > Settings, then click Extensions.

Extensions add features that need extra files. Edmund doesn’t include them, so the app stays small; you download the ones you want.

Available extensions

Advanced Math
Supports almost all KaTeX math syntax. Without it, Edmund draws math with a smaller built-in renderer that covers common formulas.
Mermaid
Draws ```mermaid code blocks as diagrams: flowcharts, state, sequence, class, entity-relationship, and XY charts. Without it, these blocks show as code.

Install and turn on an extension

  1. Click the extension in the list on the left. Extensions you haven’t installed are under Recommended.
  2. Click Download. When the download finishes, the extension moves to Installed.
  3. Click Enable.

To turn an installed extension off or on, click Disable or Enable, or click the dot next to its name. A dimmed name means the extension is off. To remove it from your Mac, click Uninstall.

When a newer version is available, an Update button appears.

If an extension has a Learn More button, click it to read more.
